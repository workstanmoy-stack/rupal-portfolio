My Personal Story

Curiosity, resilience, and the determination to keep learning.

2021 – 2022

Government & Competitive Exam Foundations

I began my professional career as a Typist at the Assam Legislative Assembly, where I developed discipline, attention to detail, and the ability to work accurately under pressure. During this period, I prepared for the APSC (Assam Public Service Combined Competitive) Examination. Through dedication, I cleared the Preliminary Examination twice and the Mains Examination once. While I came close to achieving that goal, the experience taught me perseverance, patience, and the importance of continuous self-improvement.

March 2025 – November 2025

Pivoting to Digital Marketing & Exploration

Realizing my true passion lay in creativity, strategy, and building brands, I transitioned into Digital Marketing as a Executive at Guwahati Metro Hospital. Every project strengthened my hands-on skills in social media, branding, and campaigns. In November 2025, I stepped outside my comfort zone to travel, explore new places, and experience different cultures—broadening my perspective and helping me better understand human psychology, an invaluable asset in marketing.

Present

Rising to Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

I joined ING INFOMIX as an Operations Executive. Through consistent performance, taking ownership, and driving strategic results, I was promoted to Digital Marketing Head, and eventually to Chief Marketing Officer (CMO). Today, I lead marketing strategies, oversee brand transformations, manage creative teams, and help businesses share their unique stories with the world.
