My Personal Story

Curiosity, resilience, and the determination to keep learning.

My journey has never been a straight path, it has been driven by curiosity, resilience, and the determination to keep learning.

I began my professional career as a Typist at the Assam Legislative Assembly (2021-2022), where I developed discipline, attention to detail, and the ability to work accurately under pressure. During this period, I was also preparing for the APSC (Assam Public Service Combined Competitive) Examination. Through dedication and hard work, I cleared the Preliminary Examination twice and the Mains Examination once. Although I came close to achieving that dream, the experience taught me perseverance, patience, and the importance of continuous self-improvement.

Read More ↓ Show Less ↑

As I reflected on my strengths, I realized my passion lay in creativity, strategy, and building brands. This led me to choose a new career path in Digital Marketing.

From March 2025 to November 2025, I worked as a Digital Marketing Executive, where I gained hands-on experience in social media marketing, content creation, branding, and digital campaigns. Every project strengthened my skills and confirmed that I had found the right profession.

In November 2025, I decided to step outside my comfort zone. I started travelling, exploring new places, meeting new people, and experiencing different cultures. Those experiences broadened my perspective, inspired my creativity, and helped me better understand people, an invaluable skill in marketing.

Later, I joined ING INFOMIX as an Operations Executive. Through consistent performance, a willingness to learn, and taking ownership of responsibilities, I was promoted to Digital Marketing Head, and eventually to Chief Marketing Officer (CMO). These opportunities allowed me to lead marketing strategies, oversee branding initiatives, manage creative teams, and contribute to business growth.

Beyond my professional life, I am someone who loves to sketch, create, and think differently. Creativity has always been a part of who I am, whether it's designing marketing campaigns, solving business challenges, or expressing ideas through art. I also enjoy travelling because every new destination teaches me something new and inspires fresh perspectives.

Today, I continue to learn, create, and help businesses grow through strategic marketing and innovative ideas. I believe that every challenge is an opportunity, every experience is a lesson, and every brand has a story waiting to be told.
